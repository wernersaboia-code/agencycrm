Where the opportunities are

Six product groups with good sales prospects.

  • Sustainable baby textilesorganic cotton (GOTS), OEKO-TEX bed linen, sleeping bags
  • Wooden and Montessori toyslearning towers, Pikler climbing triangles, sensory toys
  • Lightweight travel strollerscabin-size buggies, e-strollers with electric push assistance
  • i-Size car seatsolder R44 seats can no longer be sold since September 2024
  • Outdoor and alpine family productsbike trailers, snow suits, sledges
  • Children’s traditional wear (Tracht)a distinctive Austrian niche with stable seasonal demand

Suppliers from outside Europe

What must be in place before you ship.

  • An EORI number and customs clearance
  • A responsible economic operator in the EU for toys, childcare articles and electrical products
  • Import VAT of 20%
  • Warnings and instructions in German; standards such as EN 71, EN 1888 and UN R129

Indicative EU import duty

Toys, plush, puzzles0%
Prams and strollers1.7%
Car seats3.7%
Babies’ garments, knitted12%

Check TARIC for your exact product code; trade agreements can reduce the duty.
